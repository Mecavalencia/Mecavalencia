# Alejandro Valencia Suárez

### Mechatronics Engineer · Industrial Digitalization · Robotics & Automation

I'm a mechatronics engineer working where **physical systems meet software and data**. My professional experience is in industrial digitalization and manufacturing analytics: connecting machines and plant systems to data platforms, building integrations, and turning production data into tools people can use.

My work and interests come together in three areas:

- 🏭 **Industrial systems** — integrating sensors, PLCs, databases, and applications to make production processes easier to understand and improve.
- 🤖 **Robotics & mechatronics** — exploring how mechanics, electronics, control, and software come together in real-world systems.
- 🏠 **Home automation & personal projects** — coding for fun, experimenting with connected devices, and automating everyday tasks.

I enjoy understanding a system end to end: what happens in the physical process, what each signal means, where an integration can fail, and how software can make the whole thing work better. I'm also interested in systems engineering, simulation, and aerospace applications.
